How it works

Three steps to a calmer clinic.

Step 01

Set up your clinic

Add your details, consultation time, and you're live. A queue is created for you automatically.

Step 02

Patients book & get a token

Each booking issues the next token in line and confirms it to the patient right away.

Step 03

Call next, everyone updates

Tap "next patient" and every screen moves forward — no announcements, no crowding.

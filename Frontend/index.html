How it works

Three steps to a calmer clinic.

01 · Book

Patient books a slot

A patient picks a doctor and a time online, or the front desk books them in. PatientFlow issues the next token automatically.

02 · Track

Everyone follows the live queue

The patient watches their token and estimated wait update in real time, from the waiting room or from home.

03 · Get seen

Doctor calls next, everyone moves

One tap on "next patient" updates every screen at once — no announcements, no crowding, no guessing.
